*VV has no distinct letters for /j/ or /w/, which is theorised to be because these consonants were originally vowels that got reanalised during this transitional phase. There are too many inconsistencies to say for sure, though.

Special Letter

[]

The main purpose of [] is to indicate an alveolar plosive before an alveolar fricative.

Diacritics

There are two main kinds of diacritics used in VV; one is for indicating tone, and the other indicates a subsequent sound in order to save space.

Triple Letters

When a word has three consonants or vowels in a row, there are two main ways to spell them in VV; either using a double letter combined with a third letter or diacritic, or combine the "radical" forms of the letters into a single glyph.
