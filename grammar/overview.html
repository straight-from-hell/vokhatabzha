Basic Syntax

Word Order: VSO*
Article - Noun - Possessor - Postposition - Adjective
Auxiliary (modality) - Verb - Auxiliary (aspect/evidentiality) - Adverb

*In most cases. Over time proper nouns, and later all nouns, began being placed before the verb, with the appropriate pronoun suffixing the verb.

Nouns have many modifiers to create new nouns or add grammatical information

Derivational Modifiers

ModifierMeaning
-niDiminutive

Example: ma'u - plant; ma'uni - seed; ma'uán - gardener; ma'upshóo - wood

Grammatical Modifiers

ModifierMeaning
-dáungDual
-t + last vowelPlural (default)
-uènPlural (secondary)

The secondary form is used if the default plural becomes its own separate noun over time

Gender Modifiers

Most nouns in Vòkhá'ábzhá are not gendered but gender modifiers can be used to add information or to create new words

ModifierMeaning
-koFeminine
-meMasculine
-tuHealer

Vòkhá'ábzhá has a gender ternary consisting of Feminine for words related to women; Masculine for men; Healer for a class of people who have special religious power that are taught medicine by elders
